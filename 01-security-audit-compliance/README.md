In this scenario practical activity, I am conducting a security audit for the fictional toy company, Botium Toys.

They business operates from a single physical location, meaning all there assets are in one place - this place also being their storefront.
The IT department manager wants to have this security audit to see whether, considering their growth as whole, the IT department can continue to operate in their current state or make improvements to their security posture to comply with regulation.
Having the internal audit will help to improve the company's infrastructure, and help identify and mitigate weaknesses, dangers and impacts to any of their critical assets.
As they are also an online storefront, the need process online payments from customers in the E.U. and abide by regulation in concerns to that also.

My aim in conducting this audit is to simply provide an overview of the negative impacts and risks to the business as well as potential fines the company may incur due to the current state of their security posture.

~ review the IT manager's scope, goals and risk management report
~ perform an internal audit
